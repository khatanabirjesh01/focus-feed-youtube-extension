How Topic Categories Work (3-State Engine):
• Off: Neutral—no filtering on this topic.
• Allow (Strict Mode): Only videos matching at least one 'Allow' topic will show.
• Block: Any video matching this topic is hidden.
• Channel & Handle Matching: You can directly tie channels to any field by expanding it and adding channel handles (e.g. @creator) or channel names to its keywords!
⚡ Override Rule: If you block a category (e.g., Entertainment) but add a specific channel from that category to Always-Allow Channels, that channel's videos WILL STILL BE SHOWN! Always-Allow always overrides category blocks.

Always-Allow Channels 0

⭐ Supreme Override (Always Wins): Videos from channels in this list are ALWAYS displayed, overriding all other rules:
• Overrides Blocked Categories: E.g., if you set the 'Entertainment' field to Block, but you put an entertainment creator here, their videos will still be shown!
• Overrides Blocked Keywords: Shows videos even if their titles contain blocked keywords.
• Overrides Shorts Blocker: Shorts from these channels are allowed to play.
• Mutual Exclusion: Cannot add a channel here if it is currently in your Blocked Channels list.

Blocked Channels 0

🚫 Strict Channel & Collaboration Block: Immediately hides all videos from these channels:
• Collaborations: If a blocked channel collaborates with another channel (e.g., 'PJ Explained and PJ' or 'Creator A & Creator B'), that video is automatically blocked!
• Title Mentions: Videos featuring a blocked channel in the title (e.g., 'ft. Channel', 'with Channel', or '@handle') are blocked.
• Mutual Exclusion: Cannot add a channel here if it is currently in your Always-Allow list. Remove it from Always-Allow first.

Custom Blocked Keywords 0

🔤 Keyword Filter: Hides videos containing these exact words in their title, channel name, or description:
• Word-Boundary Safe: Short words like 'ass' will never block words like 'class' or 'classic'.
• Always-Allow Override: Videos from your Always-Allow channels will still show even if they contain a blocked keyword.
